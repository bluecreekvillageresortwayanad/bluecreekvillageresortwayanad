A restful hideaway

Comfort in the heart of nature.

Set among the trees around Thanjilode, Meppadi, the resort offers a relaxed base for enjoying Wayanad's forest scenery and nearby creek. Spend your day by the water, unwind in your cottage, or gather with friends around an evening campfire.
